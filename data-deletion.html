User Data Deletion

The Egg Stealing Master · Last updated: October 22, 2026

The Egg Stealing Master stores only your game progress, linked to your app-specific Facebook player ID. We do not keep any data on our own servers.

Delete your data yourself

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites (or Games) and find The Egg Stealing Master.
  3. Choose Remove. This removes the Game's access and the game data stored for it, including your saved progress.

Ask us to delete it

Open a request titled "Data deletion" at github.com/roma1501/egg-stealing-master-legal/issues (do not post personal data there; we will reply with how to verify the request). We complete deletion requests within 30 days.
