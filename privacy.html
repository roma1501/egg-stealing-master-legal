Privacy Policy

The Egg Stealing Master · Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the game The Egg Stealing Master ("the Game"), made by its independent developers ("we"), uses when you play it on Facebook.

Information we use

  • Game progress — money, pets, unlocked islands, cosmetics and settings. It is saved on your device and/or in the cloud save provided by Facebook for this game, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Your Facebook player ID — an app-specific ID provided by Facebook. It is used only to load and save your progress. We do not use your name, profile photo, friends list, e-mail address or any other profile data.

We do not run our own servers for the Game, we do not sell personal information and we do not use your data for profiling.

Advertising

The Game shows ads served through Facebook (Meta Audience Network). Meta may process data about your device and ad interactions under the Meta Privacy Policy. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook settings.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Facebook requires users to be at least 13 years old.

Keeping and deleting data

Your progress is kept until you remove the Game or ask us to delete it. See User data deletion for how to delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
